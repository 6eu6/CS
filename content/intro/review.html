Daily review

Two things come back here. Quiz questions you got wrong return tomorrow, then after 3, 7 and 14 days. Coding exercises that took you three or more tries (or the solution) return after 2 days: you solve them again from a blank editor, and after clean passes they return after 7, 21 and 60 days, then leave the list. This is spaced repetition: the most effective way known to move knowledge into long-term memory. Five minutes a day, before starting new material.
