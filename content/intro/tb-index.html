The power tools now live inside the weeks, in lecture order, so they only build on what you've learned. This page is their index. The deep dives go deep on individual tools that professionals use every day but courses mention in passing, like repr(). For each one you get every symbol explained, the exact problem it solves, real workplace scenarios, the mistakes people make, and exercises that force you to choose it yourself.

Every deep dive follows the same shape

  1. The question it answers. One sentence. If you can't say what question a tool answers, you won't think of it at the right moment.
  2. Anatomy. Tap every token.
  3. How it works, with a runner to experiment in.
  4. Real-work scenarios: situations from actual jobs (data cleaning, reports, logs, finance), the code, and why this tool beats the alternatives.
  5. Use it well: idioms professionals use, and the traps.
  6. Predict, then practise: prediction drills, interactive exercises and auto-tested problems up to "boss" level.
