Part A: helper functions

4 stepsFunctions, strings, numbers

Every finance tool needs the same small helpers: show money properly, understand amounts people type, express one number as a share of another. Build them now; you'll use them in every later part.

Why start with small functions?

Professional programs are built from small, tested pieces. money() will be called hundreds of times by later parts. If it has a bug, you fix it in one place.
