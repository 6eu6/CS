Real-world projects

3 projects4 steps eachBuilt like a real job

At work you rarely get a neat one-function exercise. You get a messy request, and you build the solution step by step, testing as you go. Each project here works that way: the steps unlock one at a time, and your code carries over from step to step, just like extending a real program.

How projects work

Read the step, extend your code, press Check. When a step passes, the next one unlocks and the task updates. Your code stays, so you build on it. Earlier steps' tests no longer apply once the output format changes; that's normal when requirements grow.
